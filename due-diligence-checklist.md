# Due Diligence Checklist

Structured checklist for Pre-IPO and late-stage VC investment due diligence.

## Financial Due Diligence

- [ ] Audited financial statements (last 2-3 years)
- [ ] Monthly P&L for trailing 12-24 months
- [ ] Balance sheet and cash flow statement
- [ ] Revenue breakdown by product, segment, geography, customer
- [ ] ARR/MRR bridge (new, expansion, contraction, churn)
- [ ] Unit economics by cohort (CAC, LTV, payback by channel)
- [ ] Burn rate trend and cash runway projection
- [ ] Cap table review (fully diluted, option pool, SAFEs, convertibles)
- [ ] Debt schedule and covenant compliance
- [ ] Revenue recognition policy and methodology
- [ ] Budget vs. actuals for last 2 years (execution accuracy)

## Commercial Due Diligence

- [ ] Customer list with revenue per customer
- [ ] Top-10 and top-20 customer concentration analysis
- [ ] Customer retention and cohort analysis (net and gross)
- [ ] Customer references (target: 5-10 interviews)
- [ ] Sales pipeline and conversion rates by stage
- [ ] Contract terms review (length, renewal, termination)
- [ ] Pricing history and pricing power assessment
- [ ] Win/loss analysis against key competitors
- [ ] Customer NPS or satisfaction data

## Market Due Diligence

- [ ] TAM/SAM/SOM analysis with verifiable assumptions
- [ ] Independent market size estimates (Gartner, IDC, etc.)
- [ ] Competitive landscape mapping and market share
- [ ] Regulatory environment scan (current and anticipated)
- [ ] Macro risk assessment (cyclicality, rates, geopolitical)
- [ ] Technology disruption risk assessment

## Technology & Product

- [ ] Product demo and feature walkthrough
- [ ] Technology architecture review
- [ ] IP portfolio (patents, trademarks, trade secrets)
- [ ] Security posture (SOC 2, ISO 27001, pen test results)
- [ ] Data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
- [ ] Product roadmap and development velocity
- [ ] Infrastructure costs and scalability limits

## Team & Organization

- [ ] Founder background verification
- [ ] Management team track records
- [ ] Org chart and key hire plan
- [ ] Employee retention and turnover rates
- [ ] Key-person dependencies identified
- [ ] Board composition and governance structure

## Legal

- [ ] Corporate structure and subsidiaries
- [ ] Material contracts review
- [ ] Outstanding or threatened litigation
- [ ] IP assignment agreements (employees and contractors)
- [ ] Related-party transactions
- [ ] Regulatory compliance status

## Investor & Governance

- [ ] Existing investor terms and protective provisions
- [ ] Liquidation preference stack and participation rights
- [ ] Anti-dilution provisions
- [ ] Board seat and observer rights
- [ ] Registration rights (relevant for Pre-IPO)
- [ ] Secondary transaction history and restrictions

## Valuation Cross-Checks

- [ ] Public comparable company analysis completed
- [ ] Precedent transaction analysis (recent private rounds)
- [ ] DCF model with sensitivity analysis
- [ ] Secondary market price verification (multiple sources)
- [ ] Entry price vs. last round step-up analysis
- [ ] Downside protection analysis (liquidation preference coverage)

---

## Completeness Scoring

| Completeness | Assessment | Action |
|-------------|------------|--------|
| 90-100% | Comprehensive | Ready for investment committee |
| 70-89% | Substantial | Prioritize remaining gaps |
| 50-69% | Partial | Material gaps; delay decision |
| Below 50% | Insufficient | Too early for investment decision |

**Critical items** (must complete before any decision):
1. Audited financials or detailed management accounts
2. Cap table and investor rights review
3. Customer concentration and retention data
4. Competitive landscape assessment
5. Public comparable multiples benchmarking
